💡 Feature selection tip

Numeric attributes such as age, purchase amount and previous purchases are used directly. Categorical attributes such as gender, category and subscription status are automatically converted into numerical representations before clustering.

Select at least two attributes. Customer ID, item names, location, color and size are not offered as primary clustering features because they are identifiers or can create unnecessarily high-dimensional segments.

✦ Clustering methods

K-Means: Partitional clustering divides customers into K groups by minimizing the distance between customers and their cluster centers.

Hierarchical: Agglomerative clustering starts with individual customers and progressively merges similar groups to form a hierarchy.

✦ How to interpret these segments

Each cluster represents customers with similar characteristics according to the attributes you selected. For example, a cluster with higher purchase amounts and frequent purchases may represent highly active customers, while a cluster with lower purchase frequency may represent occasional shoppers.

The PCA chart is a two-dimensional visualization of the higher-dimensional clustering feature space. The axes do not represent individual original attributes.
